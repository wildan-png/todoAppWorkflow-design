## Jordan Reyes — Independent consultant + rotating contractors

**Role:** Principal consultant (operations / GTM advisory); hires **project-based specialists** (researchers, slide designers) per engagement.

**Demographics:** 34 · **Chicago, IL** (remote-first; clients US/EU) · **Solo legal entity** with **0–2 contractors** active at any time · Income tied to **fixed-fee milestones**, not retainers only.

**Goals**

- Keep **every client deliverable** visible with **owner + status** without hiring an ops person.
- Let **short-term collaborators** see what they owe **without** paying for another seat on “serious PM” pricing.
- Protect **margin** on **small projects** where tooling cost would look silly on the P&L.

**Pain points**

- **Per-seat PM math** breaks when a contractor only works **two weeks** but needs **visibility**.
- **Slack + spreadsheet** drift: tasks live in threads nobody searches after Tuesday.
- Tried **Linear-class tools**: felt like **overkill** and **priced for eng teams**, not **solo + gig specialists**.

**Context of use**

- **When:** Morning triage before calls; **mid-week** checks between deliverables; **pre-milestone** syncs with contractors.
- **Where:** **Desktop browser** at home office / coworking — matches MVP **fixed-layout web** assumption.
- **How:** **Quick add** tasks, scan **list**, switch to **board** for “what’s in motion,” open **detail** for briefs and **image refs** (mockups, diagrams).

**Key behavioral traits**

- **Tech-savvy** (lives in Notion/Calendly/Stripe); **impatient with setup**; wants **defaults that look intentional** (dark, compact, professional — per founder brief).
- **Outcome-oriented:** cares about **throughput and billing**, not methodology theater.

**JTBD alignment**

- **Primary:** [JTBD **1**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (visibility without seat-tax / login juggling).
- **Strong secondary:** [JTBD **2**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (one desktop truth vs Slack/email archaeology).

## Priya Nair — Studio lead at a two-person creative shop

**Role:** Co-founder / creative director; shares delivery with **one full-time designer** and occasional **freelance motion or copy** help.

**Demographics:** 38 · **Austin, TX** · **2 FTE + sporadic freelancers** · Revenue from **brand + campaign** work with **hard client dates**.

**Goals**

- Replace **tool fatigue**: one place for **status + ownership** that doesn’t feel like **running IT**.
- Ship **client-facing professionalism**—boards that don’t look like a **throwaway template**.
- Move work **To do → In progress → Done** (per founder default statuses) without configuring **Spaces/Folders/Lists** hierarchies.

**Pain points**

- Current **all-in-one PM** (**ClickUp-style**) → **notification noise**, **slow loads**, and **days lost** to workspace structure they didn’t need.
- **Not** a software team: **sprint language** and dev-centric workflows feel alien.
- **Deadline week** chaos when **Slack** is the “system of record.”

**Context of use**

- **When:** **Daily** stand-up-style glance; **spikes** **48h before** client reviews or **invoice sends**.
- **Where:** **Desktop** in studio + WFH; heavy **visual** tasks → uses **image attachments** on tasks.
- **How:** **Board by status** for pipeline feel; **list** for catch-all scanning; **detail** for creative notes + refs; expects **list ↔ board ↔ detail** to stay **in sync**.

**Key behavioral traits**

- **Detail-oriented on deliverables**, **impatient with admin**; **visually literate**—UI density and polish matter for trust.
- Will **tolerate** donation prompts if **non-blocking** and dismissible (per brief).

**JTBD alignment**

- **Primary:** [JTBD **3**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (escape all-in-one bloat → **list + board + detail** only).
- **Strong secondary:** [JTBD **5**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (deadline / invoice crunch with cofounder + subs).

## Miguel Santos — Developer-founder with a micro product & client patches

**Role:** **Full-stack dev** running **small SaaS maintenance** plus **short client fix-it contracts**; sometimes pairs with **one other dev** or a **designer** on discrete scopes.

**Demographics:** 29 · **Lisbon, Portugal** (works US hours part of the week) · **1–3 collaborators** max · Comfortable with **GitHub** but refuses **ops overhead** for **non-code** tasking.

**Goals**

- Track **client patches** and **internal chores** in something **as fast-feeling as Linear** but **scoped** to **tasks**, not **full backlog religion**.
- Avoid **self-hosting** ops (**Plane-style** appeal intellectually, **hosting/time cost** practically).
- Keep **priority + assignee** obvious for **who pulls the next thread**.

**Pain points**

- **Engineering PM tools** tuned for **roadmaps & cycles** add **ceremony** his **2-person** reality doesn’t need.
- **Pricing** on seat-based tools stings when the **second seat** is **half-time**.
- **Spreadsheet** task lists don’t carry **status semantics** or **image proofs** cleanly.

**Context of use**

- **When:** Start of **deep work blocks**; **handoffs** to collaborator after standup on Discord; **before deploy / invoice**.
- **Where:** **Desktop browser**; **fixed layout** acceptable (brief defers mobile polish).
- **How:** **Keyboard-friendly** expectations (WCAG-style targets per brief); **list** for inventory, **board** for **wip limits by column**, **detail** for **notes + screenshots**.

**Key behavioral traits**

- **Very tech-savvy**; **speed-sensitive** (latency = context loss); **skeptical of AI/agent** in V1 (aligned with **no in-app agent**).
- Values **control** and **simple mental model** over configurability.

**JTBD alignment**

- **Primary:** [JTBD **4**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (non-enterprise micro-team wanting **issue-like clarity** without **dev-team ceremony**).
- **Strong secondary:** [JTBD **2**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (single desktop view each morning) and [JTBD **5**](user-research.md#jobs-to-be-done) (pre-deadline alignment with partner).
