## USP Statement
"Untuk tim freelancer kecil (1–3 orang) yang butuh koordinasi harian tanpa overhead, aTodo adalah aplikasi manajemen tugas sederhana yang memberi visibilitas status dan ownership paling cepat lewat list + board + detail task yang fokus pada eksekusi, tidak seperti ClickUp yang cenderung berat, kompleks, dan membuat tim kecil membayar serta menavigasi fitur yang tidak mereka pakai."

## Differentiation Angles
- Activation jauh lebih cepat: alur inti dibatasi ke satu workspace, tiga status default (`To do`, `In progress`, `Done`), dan satu model task inti; bisa diuji dengan metrik *time-to-first-task* dan *time-to-first-status-move* vs ClickUp/Plane.
- Ekonomi kolaborasi lebih fair untuk micro-team: core workflow tetap usable tanpa paywall dan tanpa ketergantungan add-on AI; bisa diuji dengan membandingkan kebutuhan upgrade berbayar untuk loop dasar (create-assign-move-done).
- Scope sengaja anti-bloat: produk tidak membawa docs/wiki, automations, dashboard berat, atau agent in-app pada V1; bisa diverifikasi langsung dari surface area fitur yang tersedia.

## Why This USP Wins
USP ini menyerang titik lemah kompetitor yang paling konsisten di riset: kompleksitas onboarding/workspace design (terutama ClickUp), beban fitur yang tidak relevan untuk tim kecil, dan friksi harga berbasis seat/add-on. Pain point user paling nyata adalah kehilangan waktu billable karena harus “mengelola tool” dulu sebelum mengelola pekerjaan. Di saat yang sama, ada feature gap yang belum dipenuhi dengan baik oleh Linear/Plane/ClickUp untuk niche ini: pengalaman status + owner yang cepat, tenang, dan right-sized untuk workflow freelance harian. Dengan memusatkan produk pada loop inti saja (capture → assign → progress → done), aTodo menang lewat kecepatan operasional, bukan kedalaman suite.

## English Version
"For small freelance teams (1–3 people) that need daily coordination without overhead, aTodo is a simple task management app that delivers the fastest visibility into status and ownership through a focused list + board + task detail flow built for execution, unlike ClickUp which tends to be heavy, complex, and forces small teams to pay for and navigate features they do not use."

### Differentiation Angles
- Faster activation: the core flow is limited to one workspace, three default statuses (`To do`, `In progress`, `Done`), and one core task model; this can be tested with *time-to-first-task* and *time-to-first-status-move* metrics vs ClickUp/Plane.
- Fairer collaboration economics for micro-teams: the core workflow remains usable without a paywall and without dependency on AI add-ons; this can be tested by comparing paid upgrade requirements for the basic loop (create-assign-move-done).
- Deliberately anti-bloat scope: the product excludes docs/wiki, automations, heavy dashboards, and in-app agents in V1; this can be directly verified from the available feature surface area.

### Why This USP Wins
This USP targets the most consistent competitor weaknesses in research: onboarding/workspace design complexity (especially in ClickUp), feature overload that is irrelevant for small teams, and seat/add-on pricing friction. The clearest user pain point is losing billable time because they have to "manage the tool" before managing the work. At the same time, there is a feature gap not yet addressed well by Linear/Plane/ClickUp for this niche: a fast, calm, and right-sized status + owner experience for daily freelance workflows. By centering the product on the core loop only (capture → assign → progress → done), aTodo wins through operational speed, not suite depth.
